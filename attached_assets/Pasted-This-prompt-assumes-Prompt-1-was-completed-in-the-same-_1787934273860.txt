Rules:

Stable keys never change, even if display names change.

Referenced values cannot be deleted.

Inactive values remain available for historical reporting but cannot be selected for new campaigns.

Additions and changes must be recorded in the audit history.

Support parent-child structures where applicable, such as Segment to Subsegment and Product Family to Product.

Support many-to-many mappings, such as Persona to Segment and Product to Customer Need.
